About

Eagle Cabin

Eagle Cabin sits up among the spruce, with plenty of room for family or friends to settle in together. With a queen bed, two double beds and space for six, it’s a comfortable home base for a few days spent exploring Anahim Lake and the country beyond.

Outside, the long deck puts you right among the trees and makes a pretty hard place to beat for a morning coffee while the light comes through the spruce. Inside, you’ll have power, a fridge, wood stove and running water during the summer.

The cabin also has its own attached indoor pit toilet, with showers available at the separate wash cabin near the Heritage cabins.
