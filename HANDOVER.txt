THE RULE, which matters more than filling empty slots:
A photograph belongs ONLY to the accommodation the owner names
when uploading it. Never guess from appearance, never borrow one
room's photograph for another, never use one as a placeholder.
Accommodations without photographs show a neutral "photographs
coming" frame instead.

   clearly show a wall-mounted television. No TV is listed for ANY
   accommodation at present, because the old site's blanket claim
   for "suites" turned out to be unreliable. Likely answer: the

   needs confirming before it goes on the site.

2. Coffee makers. Visible in every suite photographed. Not listed.

3. Electric heaters / fireplaces. Visible in Chilcotin and Tucson.
   Not listed. Worth advertising for shoulder season.

4. An upright piano appears in Tucson and IS mentioned in its
   description. Confirm that is wanted.

5. Pelican's bed looks wider than a twin in the photograph, but
   the specification says twin. Worth checking.

6. Camping: number of sites, hookups, facilities, occupancy,
   whether trailers are welcome. One Camping entry exists with a
   photograph and no invented detail.

7. Do the cabins have Wi-Fi? Only the suites are listed with it.

8. Descriptions for Green (the only suite without one written from
   its photographs).

9. info@eaglesnest-resort.com has never been verified. The booking
   form composes mail to it. If it is wrong, every enquiry
   bounces. This is the most important item on this list.

DEPLOYMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------
Upload the whole folder, not just the .html files. css, js and
images must go up together.

The stylesheet address carries a hash of the css and js content
(?v=ffe03361 at the time of writing), so it changes only when
those files change. _headers tells Cloudflare to revalidate css
and js on every request.

If the site ever looks unstyled after a deploy, that is almost
always an old stylesheet still being served. Open css/site.css on
the live URL and search it for ".accom" — if it is not there, the
stylesheet did not upload.
