Where this is

On Highway 20, between Williams Lake and Bella Coola

Highway 20 runs from the Cariboo across the Chilcotin plateau and drops to the sea at Bella Coola. Anahim Lake sits roughly two-thirds of the way along, the natural place to break the drive before The Hill.

Out the door

Things to do around Anahim Lake

Paddling, fishing, hiking and birding through the summer. Sled, ski and snowshoe once the snow settles.

Summer Paddle · fish · hike

  • At the water's edge

    Canoes & pedal boats

    Grab a paddle and head out from the dock. Canoes and pedal boats are available for guests to explore Anahim Lake at their own pace.

    More on paddling

  • Off the dock

    Fishing

    The fishing starts where the dock ends. Bring your own gear, take a boat out, or cast from the shore and see what happens.

    More on fishing

  • Tweedsmuir Park

    The Rainbow Range

    West along Highway 20, inside Tweedsmuir Park, the Rainbow Range is the big one: old volcanic rock streaked red, orange and yellow, with the alpine arriving sooner than you expect.

    More on hiking

  • Follow the water

    Hunlen & Hotnarko Falls

    Hunlen Falls is farther into Tweedsmuir Park and a longer day. The Hotnarko River drops off the edge of the Precipice, a canyon walled in basalt. Ask Diane and Murray about the way in.

Winter Sled · skate · fish

  • Miles of it

    Snowmobiling

    The West Chilcotin is sledding country, and there is a great deal of it: miles of backcountry once the snow is down. Come with your own machines and talk to Diane and Murray about where people have been going.

    More on winter

  • Through the ice

    Ice fishing

    The same lake, a good deal harder. Drill a hole, set up and wait it out. Check the regulations before you go, and check the ice before you walk on it.

    More on the ice

  • When it takes

    Skate the lake

    When conditions permit, there is nothing quite like skating out on a frozen lake. Conditions decide, not the calendar. Ask before you go out.

Keep your eyes open

Who's around?

Deer along the treeline, bears working the berry patches, and birds on the water all summer. Most of it turns up without being looked for.

American white pelican

Lake & wetlands

Common on Anahim Lake in the warmer months, these impressive birds are often seen gliding, fishing and gathering in groups.

Red fox

Forest edges & open country

Often seen around forest edges and open country, foxes are most active around dawn and dusk.

Snowshoe hare

Forest & brush

Look for them in the forest and along the trails. Their coats change with the seasons, helping them blend from brown to white.

Also keep an eye out for

  • Black bear forests & berry patches
  • Mule deer meadows & valleys
  • Moose wetlands & lakeshores
  • Bald eagle along the lake & rivers
  • Osprey lakes & wetlands
  • Great grey owl forest edges & clearings

Wild country

Wildlife here is just that: wild. Give animals plenty of space, never feed them, and ask your hosts about recent sightings before heading out.

The journey

Four ways to reach Anahim Lake

Anahim Lake is remote, but it is not hard to get to. Tell us which way you are coming so we know when to expect you.

  • By car

    West from Williams Lake on Highway 20, about 3½ hours across the Chilcotin plateau. We're one kilometre off the highway at Anahim Lake. Coming the other way, it's roughly 2 hours up from Bella Coola, including The Hill.

  • By plane

    Anahim Lake Airport is a twelve-minute drive from our gate. Fly in, and we'll take it from there. Ask about a pickup when you book. Schedules change seasonally, so check current service before you commit to dates.

  • By floatplane

    Float-equipped aircraft can land directly on Anahim Lake and come ashore at the property, with no shuttle and no rental car. Murray flies, so you'll be talking to someone who understands the question. Call ahead and we'll meet you at the water.

  • By ferry, via Bella Coola

    Sail the Central Coast with BC Ferries to Bella Coola, then drive east on Highway 20 through the valley and up The Hill. The long way round, and the beautiful one, about 2 hours from the terminal.
