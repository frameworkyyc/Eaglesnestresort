There’s a lot out here.

Paddle out from the dock. Watch the pelicans come in. Cast a line, find a trail or head farther into the Chilcotin. And when the lake freezes, come back and do it all differently.

On foot

Find a trail.

The country around Anahim Lake opens up quickly once you are walking in it.

The Rainbow Range

The Rainbow Range

West along Highway 20, inside Tweedsmuir Park, the Rainbow Range is the big one — old volcanic rock streaked red, orange and yellow, with the alpine arriving sooner than you expect. It is the regional hike people come back talking about.

On the way in

The Beef Trail

The walk in crosses rock shelves and still green water before it reaches the falls. Ask Diane and Murray before you set off — they will tell you what the road is doing.

Follow the water

Chase a waterfall.

South-west of here

Hotnarko Falls

The Hotnarko River drops off the edge of the Precipice, a canyon walled in basalt. It is the kind of place that makes the drive out worth it on its own.

Farther into the park

Hunlen Falls

Farther into Tweedsmuir Park, and a longer day than the others. Ask Diane and Murray about the way in before you plan it.

Keep your eyes open

Who’s around?

Deer along the treeline, bears working the berry patches, and birds on the water all summer. Most of it turns up without being looked for.

American white pelican

Lake & wetlands

Common on Anahim Lake in the warmer months, these impressive birds are often seen gliding, fishing and gathering in groups.

Red fox

Forest edges & open country

Often seen around forest edges and open country, foxes are most active around dawn and dusk.

Snowshoe hare

Forest & brush

Look for them in the forest and along the trails. Their coats change with the seasons, helping them blend from brown to white.

Miles of it

Snowmobile

The West Chilcotin is sledding country, and there is a great deal of it — miles of backcountry once the snow is down. Come with your own machines and talk to Diane and Murray about where people have been going.

Ice fish
