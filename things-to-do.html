Anahim Lake · West Chilcotin

There’s a lot out here.

Paddle out from the dock. Watch the pelicans come in. Cast a line, find a trail or head farther into the Chilcotin. And when the lake freezes, come back and do it all differently.

On foot

Find a trail.

The country around Anahim Lake opens up quickly once you are walking in it.

The Rainbow Range

West along Highway 20, inside Tweedsmuir Park, the Rainbow Range is the big one — old volcanic rock streaked red, orange and yellow, with the alpine arriving sooner than you expect. It is the regional hike people come back talking about.

The Beef Trail

The walk in crosses rock shelves and still green water before it reaches the falls. Ask Diane and Murray before you set off — they will tell you what the road is doing.

Follow the water

Chase a waterfall.

Hotnarko Falls

South-west of here the Hotnarko River drops off the edge of the Precipice, a canyon walled in basalt. It is the kind of place that makes the drive out worth it on its own.

Hunlen Falls

Farther into Tweedsmuir Park, and a longer day than the others. Ask Diane and Murray about the way in before you plan it.

Keep your eyes open

Who’s around?

Deer along the treeline, bears working the berry patches, and birds on the water all summer. Most of it turns up without being looked for.

American White Pelican Pelecanus erythrorhynchos
Bald Eagle Haliaeetus leucocephalus
Osprey Pandion haliaetus
Black Tern Chlidonias niger
Great Grey Owl Strix nebulosa

Bird watching

Eagles work the shoreline, ospreys fish the shallows and terns turn over the open water — most of it visible from the dock or a canoe.

The regulars

American white pelicans feed on the lake around Eagle’s Nest. Look straight across from the lodge — that is usually where they are.

No car required

Start at the dock.

Some of the best of it is thirty seconds from your door.

Canoes & pedal boats

Grab a paddle and head out from the dock. Canoes and pedal boats are available for guests to explore Anahim Lake at their own pace.

See what’s biting.

The fishing starts where the dock ends. Bring your own gear, take a boat out, or cast from the shore and see what happens.

Anahim Lake fish

  • Rainbow Trout
  • Cuttbow

Licences, limits and seasons change. Check the B.C. freshwater fishing regulations before you go.

When the lake freezes…

Winter doesn’t shut the place down.

Snowmobile

The West Chilcotin is sledding country, and there is a great deal of it — miles of backcountry once the snow is down. Come with your own machines and talk to Diane and Murray about where people have been going.

On the ice

Out on the lake.

Ice fish

The same lake, a good deal harder. Drill a hole, set up and wait it out. Check the regulations before you go, and check the ice before you walk on it.

Skate the lake

When conditions permit, there’s nothing quite like skating out on a frozen lake. Conditions decide, not the calendar — ask before you go out.

One more thing

Not sure where to start?

Ask Diane and Murray. They live here.
