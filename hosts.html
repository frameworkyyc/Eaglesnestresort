About us

Diane & Murray, with Oliver

Diane and Murray are the new owners of Eagle’s Nest. Drawn to the outdoors and always ready for the next trip, they’ve found a fitting home on the shore of Anahim Lake.

Murray is a pilot, and they keep their airplane at Anahim Lake Airport, only a stone’s skip away.

Along for the adventure is Oliver, their Westie, who has appointed himself unofficial host and takes greeting arrivals very seriously.
