Where this is

The only stretch of road like it in Canada

Highway 20 runs from the Cariboo across the Chilcotin plateau and drops to the sea at Bella Coola. Anahim Lake sits roughly two-thirds of the way along — the natural place to break the drive before The Hill.

The lodge

Rooms, cabins and a table by the water

Eleven places to sleep, from lakeside suites to hand-built log cabins with a wood stove and firewood on the porch. Meals are cooked here and served under the timber trusses, with the lake filling the windows.
