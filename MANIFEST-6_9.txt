TWO THINGS TO CHECK WHEN YOU LOOK AT IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE ANGLE. "Ten degrees clockwise" was read as ten degrees FROM
   WHERE IT WAS. The card sat at -6, so it is now +4 — it has come
   through upright and now leans slightly the other way. If you
   meant an absolute +10, that is a further six degrees and takes a
   moment to change. Worth a look before deciding.

2. THE HOOK'S OLD CORNER IS NOW EMPTY. It used to fill the space
   below the fish card on the right. With the hook moved up under
   the heading, the bottom right of the section is open ground. That
   follows from the move rather than being a fault, but it is the
   most obvious change to the balance and you may want something
   there — or may prefer it open.
