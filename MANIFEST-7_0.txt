TWO THINGS WORTH KNOWING
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE 15% NEEDED A WIDER COLUMN SPAN, NOT JUST A BIGGER MAX-WIDTH.
   The trout's four-column span was capping it below its own
   max-width, so raising the max-width alone did nothing — the first
   attempt measured +0.0%. It now spans five columns and the growth
   is real at every viewport. This is the third graphic on this page
   caught by that same cap.

2. 14 DEGREES TOOK THE CARD OFF THE LEFT EDGE ON PHONES. The corner
   swings about 13% of the card's own width past its box, which put
   it outside the viewport between 320 and 600px. The mobile rule
   now carries an inline margin to pay for the swing. The card is
   NOT resized — it is the same width it was, just seated far enough
   in for its corner to fit.

   The handnote styling is deliberately KEPT in the stylesheet even
   though nothing uses it now, because "Trails lead to new stories."
   is still waiting for a home and will want it.
