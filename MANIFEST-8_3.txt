whatever sits below it has to be a band-2 section. I put the gallery
in as a plain section, so the colour stepped from rgb(231,224,206)
to rgb(241,236,223) right at the base of the wave — a hard line
across the full width.

The gallery is now band-2. The section that follows it is band-2 as
well, so the two simply run on as one continuous band with nothing
visible between them.

Measured at the two joins, at 375, 1024, 1440 and 1920:

    wave into gallery        step 0.0
    gallery into next        step 0.0

My mistake, and an avoidable one: the bands on this site alternate
and the wave dividers are coloured to match their neighbours, so any
new section has to be checked against the divider above it and the
section below it. That is now written into the handover.
