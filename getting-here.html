Getting here

Four ways to reach the lake

Anahim Lake is remote, but it is not hard to get to. Pick whichever of these suits your trip — and tell us which one you're taking, so we know when to expect you.

The property from the air · the dock runs straight out into the lake

01

By car

West from Williams Lake on Highway 20, about 3½ hours across the Chilcotin plateau. We're one kilometre off the highway at Anahim Lake.

Coming the other way, it's roughly 2 hours up from Bella Coola, including The Hill.

Hwy 20 · 1 km off the road

02

By plane

Anahim Lake Airport is a twelve-minute drive from our gate. Fly in, and we'll take it from there — ask about a pickup when you book.

Schedules change seasonally, so check current service before you commit to dates.

YAA · 12 minutes away

03

By floatplane

Float-equipped aircraft can land directly on Anahim Lake and come ashore at the property — no shuttle, no rental car.

Murray flies, so you'll be talking to someone who understands the question. Call ahead and we'll meet you at the water.

Straight onto the lake

04

By ferry, via Bella Coola

Sail the Central Coast with BC Ferries to Bella Coola, then drive east on Highway 20 through the valley and up The Hill.

The long way round, and the beautiful one — about 2 hours from the terminal.

Inside Passage · then Hwy 20

Straight onto the water

If you fly, this is the short version: land on Anahim Lake, taxi in, and tie up at our dock. You are a two-minute walk from the dining room.

Call ahead so we know to expect you, and we’ll meet you at the water.
