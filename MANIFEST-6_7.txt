TWO THINGS TO BE AWARE OF
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE SECTION IS TALLER AGAIN. The corners need room and the grid
   does not know they exist, so the space has to be bought with
   margin. Measured section height:

              before 6.6      6.6      now      vs 6.6
      900px         1269     1267     1309        +42
     1100px         1483     1429     1499        +70
     1280px         1720     1605     1687        +82
     1440px         1838     1716     1801        +85
     1920px         2021     1900     1983        +83

   Still shorter than it was before the 6.6 tightening at 1280px and
   above; slightly taller at 900-1100px, where there was no slack to
   give in the first place. If the height matters more than the
   angles, reducing the fox from -20 to about -12 gives back roughly
   half of it — that print is the expensive one.

2. NOTHING ROTATES BELOW 900px. The prints there run to 64% of a
   narrow column with only 4.5vw of page padding, and a 20 degree
   tilt would swing a corner off the screen. Mobile and tablet are
   byte-identical to 6.5. Say the word and it can be done properly
   for narrow screens, with the print inset to pay for the swing.
