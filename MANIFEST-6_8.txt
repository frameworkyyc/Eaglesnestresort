THREE THINGS I COULD NOT DO, AND WHY
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE HOOK ONLY ROSE ABOUT 10px. It shares columns 10-11 with the
   fish card. The card is turned -6deg, so its low corner is its
   bottom LEFT and its right edge rides about 30px higher — but the
   hook still meets that right edge. A larger lift was tried and
   buried the hook 29px inside the card. It cannot go further right
   either: the lake sheet is decorated down both outer fifths and
   nothing on this spread may pass column 12. What makes the hook
   stop looking stranded is the lake note and the handnote coming up
   level with it, which they now do.

2. THE FISHING GROUP COULD NOT RISE FURTHER. Its ceiling is the
   CARD, not the trout — the card sits under the canoe drawing and
   cannot pass its foot. The trout could have risen about 240px,
   since only the heading block is above it, but that would have
   left the group behind, which is the opposite of what was asked.
   The trout and the note were brought DOWN to the card instead. The
   open ground on the left, between the intro and the trout, is the
   cost of keeping the four fishing pieces together.

3. THE GAP BEFORE THE MOUNTAINS WAS NEVER THERE TO CUT. The ridge
   sheet's first haze already sits about 14px under the last line of
   the lake note. Trimming the section's bottom padding pulls the
   next section up and takes the mountain sheet with it, straight
   onto the copy. The mountains start sooner in this release only
   because the content came up and the section is shorter.

   If you want them higher still, the honest lever is the fish
   card's column: moving it out from under the canoe drawing would
   free the whole fishing group to rise, at the cost of changing the
   staggered arrangement you already approved. Worth a look together
   before anyone does it.
